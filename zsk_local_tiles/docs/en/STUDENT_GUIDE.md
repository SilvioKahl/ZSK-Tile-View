# Guide for students

**Plugin:** ZSK course tiles (`local_zsk_local_tiles`)

---

## What changes

Courses are often shown as **tiles** — with image, title, and short text — instead of a text-only list.

Examples:

- **Dashboard**
- **My courses**
- optionally **Site home** or **course catalogue**

---

## Enter a course

**Click the tile** — you go straight into the course.

---

## Notes at the bottom of a tile

You may see, for example:

- how far you have progressed
- whether you are not yet enrolled (when browsing the catalogue)

No extra settings are required on your part.

---

## Course missing?

- Check **My courses** and search
- Contact support if an enrolled course is missing — that is not caused by the tile view itself

# Guide for teachers

**Plugin:** ZSK course tiles (`local_zsk_local_tiles`)

---

## What you see

When enabled by your institution, courses appear as **tiles** instead of a plain list — with course image, title, and short description.

Typical locations:

- **Dashboard** (`/my/`)
- **My courses**
- optionally **Site home** and **course catalogue** (premium)

---

## Open a course

Click the tile — you enter the course as usual.

---

## Footer information (premium)

The bottom of a tile may show:

- learning progress / completion status
- “not yet enrolled” (catalogue)
- number of courses in a category

---

## Image and text on the tile

Your institution chooses in the plugin settings **where** image and preview text come from:

### Option A – Course settings (common)

Maintain image and description as usual in the course:

1. Open the course → **Settings**
2. Upload a **course image**
3. Edit the **course summary** (about 300 characters are shown on the tile)

### Option B – Separate upload

If administration selected “Separate upload” and authorised you:

1. Open **“Maintain tile content”** in the navigation
2. Choose **Maintain course tiles** / a category
3. Save a dedicated tile image and preview text there

These contents are **independent** of the course form and are used on tiles when the switch is set to “Separate upload”.

---

## What you do not configure

Layout and the content source switch (course settings vs. separate upload) are managed by **site administrators**. Contact Moodle support for display issues.
